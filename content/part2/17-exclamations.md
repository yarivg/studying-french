# Exclamations

An exclamation is a statement with the volume turned up: *this boat is beautiful* becomes *how
beautiful this boat is*. French does it with a small closed set of words, and the trap is that
none of them behave like their English translations. **Quel** takes a noun, **que** and
**comme** take a whole clause, and unlike a question, **nothing ever inverts**.

## The two machines

Everything below is one of two structures. Choose by what you are intensifying.

| You are stressing | Machine | Example |
|---|---|---|
| an **adjective** or **adverb** | *que* / *comme* + clause | *Comme ce bateau est beau !* |
| a **noun** | *quel* + noun | *Quel beau bateau !* |

Both say the same thing about the same boat. They differ only in which word carries the weight.

## Machine 1: que and comme + a full clause

Take the statement, put **que** or **comme** in front, change nothing else.

**Que / Comme + sujet + verbe + le reste !**

::: examples
Ce bateau est beau. → Que ce bateau est beau ! || the word order never moves
Ce bateau est beau. → Comme ce bateau est beau ! || same sentence, same meaning
Il court vite. → Comme il court vite ! || works on an adverb too
Tu as changé ! → Que tu as changé ! || and on a compound tense
Cette maison est grande. → Comme cette maison est grande ! ||
:::

::: rule Never invert after que or comme
This is the single most common mistake, because the question forms are so close.

*Comme **il est** gentil !* - not *comme est-il gentil*.
*Que **tu es** drôle !* - not *que es-tu drôle*.

A question inverts, an exclamation does not. *Que fais-tu ?* is a question; *Que tu es drôle !*
is an exclamation, and the verb stays put.
:::

### que or comme?

They mean the same thing and fill the same slot, so most of the time the choice is register:
**comme** is the neutral default, **que** is a shade more literary and lands best on short fixed
phrases - *Que c'est beau !*, *Que je suis bête !* A long clause reads better with *comme*.

Two places where only one of them works:

::: rule comme embeds under another verb, que does not
*Tu sais **comme** il est têtu.* - *you know how stubborn he is.*
*Tu vois **comme** c'est difficile.*

*Tu sais **que** il est têtu* is a different sentence altogether: *que* there means *that*, and
reports a fact rather than exclaiming about it.
:::

::: rule Only que takes a quantity
*Que de monde !* is possible; *comme de monde* is not. See **que de** below.
:::

### The same exclamation in four registers

All four fill exactly the same slot. Swap them freely; only the register changes.

| Form | Register |
|---|---|
| *Que ce bateau est beau !* | soutenu, written |
| *Comme ce bateau est beau !* | courant, always safe |
| *Ce que ce bateau est beau !* | familier, spoken |
| *Qu'est-ce que ce bateau est beau !* | familier, very spoken |

::: examples
Que cette histoire est triste ! || formal
Comme cette histoire est triste ! || neutral
Ce que cette histoire est triste ! || casual
Qu'est-ce que cette histoire est triste ! || casual, heavier
:::

::: warning qu'est-ce que has two lives
*Qu'est-ce que tu fais ?* is a question - *what are you doing?*
*Qu'est-ce que tu es drôle !* is an exclamation - *you are so funny!*

The difference is what follows. A question leaves a gap where the object should be; an
exclamation is already complete and only adds intensity.
:::

## Machine 2: quel + a noun

**Quel** is an adjective, so it agrees with the noun it exclaims about. It replaces the
article: nothing goes between *quel* and its noun except an adjective.

| | Singulier | Pluriel |
|---|---|---|
| Masculin | quel | quels |
| Féminin | quelle | quelles |

::: examples
Quel beau bateau ! || What a beautiful boat!
Quelle belle maison ! || What a beautiful house!
Quels jolis yeux ! || What lovely eyes!
Quelles belles photos ! || What beautiful photos!
Quelle chance ! || What luck! - no adjective needed
Quel dommage ! || What a shame!
Quelle horreur ! || How awful!
:::

::: warning No article after quel
English says *what **a** boat*. French does not: the *a* is already inside *quel*.

*Quel bateau !* - not *quel un bateau*.
And *quel* cannot stand alone: *Quel beau !* is not a sentence. It needs its noun.
:::

A full clause can hang off it:

::: examples
Quel bateau tu as acheté ! || What a boat you bought!
Quelle chance tu as ! || How lucky you are!
Quelle journée j'ai passée ! || What a day I've had!
:::

::: warning quel and qu'elle sound identical
They are different machines and only the spelling separates them.

*Quelle belle maison !* - *quelle* + noun.
*Qu'elle est belle !* - *que* + the pronoun *elle* + verb.

Ask what follows: a noun means *quelle*, a verb means *qu'elle*.
:::

## Machine 3: que de + a quantity

For *so much* or *so many*, **que de** goes straight onto the noun, with no article.

::: examples
Que de monde ! || So many people!
Que de bruit ! || What a racket!
Que d'efforts ! || So much effort!
Que de temps perdu ! || So much wasted time!
:::

Formal and slightly literary. In speech you would say *Il y a tellement de monde !* instead.

## Machine 4: an intensifier inside a normal sentence

No special syntax at all: keep the sentence and add **si**, **tellement** or **vraiment**.

::: examples
C'est si beau ! || It's so beautiful!
C'est tellement beau ! || same, more spoken
Il est vraiment gentil ! || He's really kind!
Il y a tellement de monde ! || There are so many people! - tellement de + noun
J'ai tellement mangé ! || I ate so much! - tellement after the verb
:::

::: rule si and tellement for qualities, tellement de for quantities
*si grand*, *tellement grand* - an adjective.
*tellement de monde*, *tellement de travail* - a noun, so *de* comes back.

*si de monde* does not exist. This is the same split as *aussi* against *autant de* in the
comparative.
:::

## Putting the noun back at the end

Very French, and very common in speech: exclaim with a pronoun, then name the thing after a
comma. The pronoun and the noun refer to the same thing.

::: examples
Qu'il est beau, ce bateau ! || that boat, how beautiful it is
Comme elle est grande, ta sœur ! || your sister, how tall she is
Qu'elle est bonne, cette soupe ! ||
C'est beau, la mer ! ||
:::

## Choosing, in one table

| Meaning | Form |
|---|---|
| How beautiful this boat is! | *Comme ce bateau est beau !* |
| What a beautiful boat! | *Quel beau bateau !* |
| So many boats! | *Que de bateaux !* |
| It's so beautiful! | *C'est tellement beau !* |
| That boat is so beautiful! | *Qu'il est beau, ce bateau !* |

::: note The exclamation already intensifies
*Que c'est très beau !* is wrong twice over: *que* is doing the work that *très* would do.
Pick one. *Que c'est beau !* or *C'est très beau !*
:::
