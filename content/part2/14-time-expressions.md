# Depuis, pendant, il y a, pour

English collapses four different French ideas into the word *for*. Sorting them out is mostly a
matter of asking two questions: **is it still going on?** and **is the duration a span or a
point?**

## The four, in one table

| French | Meaning | Tense it takes | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **depuis** | since / for — still going | **present** (or imperfect) | *J'habite ici **depuis** 3 ans.* |
| **pendant** | for / during — a completed span | any, usually past | *J'ai vécu là **pendant** 3 ans.* |
| **il y a** | ago — a point in the past | **past** | *Je suis parti **il y a** 3 ans.* |
| **pour** | for — an *intended* span | future or past | *Je pars **pour** 3 ans.* |

::: rule depuis takes the present
This is the rule that most often catches English speakers.

English: *I **have lived** here for three years.* — present perfect.
French: *J'**habite** ici depuis trois ans.* — plain present.

The logic: you still live there, so the action is present. Using a past tense would mean you
have stopped.
:::

## depuis

Two things follow it, with different meanings:

| Followed by | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| a **duration** | for | *depuis trois ans* — for three years |
| a **point in time** | since | *depuis 2019*, *depuis lundi* — since 2019, since Monday |

::: examples
J'habite ici depuis trois ans. || I've lived here for three years.
J'habite ici depuis 2019. || I've lived here since 2019.
Je t'attends depuis une heure. || I've been waiting an hour.
Il ne fume plus depuis janvier. || He hasn't smoked since January.
:::

### depuis in the past

Shift the verb to the **imperfect** and the whole frame moves back:

::: examples
J'attendais depuis vingt minutes. || I had been waiting for twenty minutes.
Il habitait là depuis 2010. || He had been living there since 2010.
:::

### depuis with a negative

A negative with *depuis* usually takes the **passé composé**, because the thing has not
happened at all:

::: examples
Je ne l'ai pas vu depuis trois ans. || I haven't seen him for three years.
Elle n'a pas travaillé depuis janvier. || She hasn't worked since January.
:::

## pendant

A **completed** span, with a clear beginning and end. The action is over.

::: examples
J'ai vécu à Paris pendant trois ans. || I lived in Paris for three years — I don't now.
Il a dormi pendant huit heures. || He slept for eight hours.
Pendant les vacances, on a beaucoup marché. || During the holidays…
Je travaille pendant la journée. || I work during the day.
:::

::: warning pendant can be omitted
*J'ai vécu à Paris trois ans* is perfectly natural — French often drops *pendant* before a bare
duration. You can never drop *depuis* or *il y a*.
:::

**Pendant que** is the conjunction version, joining two clauses:
*Je lis **pendant que** tu cuisines.*

## il y a

A **point** in the past — English *ago*. It requires a past tense.

::: examples
Je suis parti il y a trois ans. || I left three years ago.
Je l'ai vu il y a une heure. || I saw him an hour ago.
Il y a longtemps. || A long time ago.
:::

::: warning il y a vs depuis — the clearest contrast
***Il y a** trois ans, j'ai déménagé.* — three years **ago** I moved. One event, finished.
***Depuis** trois ans, j'habite ici.* — **for** three years I've lived here. Still true.

The tense gives it away every time: *il y a* takes the past, *depuis* takes the present.
:::

### il y a … que

There is a third pattern that behaves exactly like *depuis*:

::: examples
Il y a trois ans que j'habite ici. || I've lived here for three years.
Ça fait trois ans que j'habite ici. || Same meaning; more common in speech.
Voilà trois ans que j'habite ici. || Same again; slightly literary.
:::

All four are equivalent:
*J'habite ici **depuis** trois ans* = ***Il y a** trois ans **que** j'habite ici* = ***Ça fait**
trois ans **que** j'habite ici* = ***Voilà** trois ans **que** j'habite ici.*

## pour

An **intended** or **planned** duration — the length something is *meant* to last, usually with
verbs of going and coming.

::: examples
Je pars pour trois jours. || I'm leaving for three days — that's the plan.
Il est venu pour une semaine. || He came for a week.
Nous sommes ici pour deux mois. || We're here for two months.
:::

::: warning Do not use pour for a completed duration
*J'ai vécu à Paris **pour** trois ans* is wrong. Because the time was actually spent, it needs
**pendant** — *j'ai vécu à Paris pendant trois ans*.

Reserve *pour* for planned or projected time.
:::

## dans and en

Two more that English also merges.

| French | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **dans** | in — after that much time from now | *Je pars **dans** une heure.* — in an hour's time |
| **en** | in — the time something takes | *Je l'ai fait **en** une heure.* — it took me an hour |

::: examples
Je pars dans dix minutes. || I'm leaving in ten minutes.
J'ai fini le travail en dix minutes. || I finished the work in ten minutes.
Le train arrive dans une heure. || The train arrives in an hour.
On y va en une heure. || It takes an hour to get there.
:::

## Other useful time markers

| French | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **dès** | from, as early as — *dès demain*, *dès son arrivée* |
| **jusqu'à** | until — *jusqu'à demain* |
| **à partir de** | from … onwards — *à partir de lundi* |
| **avant / après** | before / after — *avant le cours* |
| **vers** | around — *vers midi* |
| **entre … et** | between — *entre 8 et 9 heures* |
| **du … au** | from … to — *du lundi au jeudi* |
| **lors de** | at the time of — *lors de la réunion* |
| **au bout de** | after (a period had passed) — *au bout de deux heures* |
| **désormais** | from now on |
| **entre-temps** | in the meantime |

::: examples
Dès demain, je commence. || Starting tomorrow.
Du lundi au jeudi. || Monday to Thursday.
Au bout de deux heures, il est parti. || After two hours, he left.
Je travaille de 8 heures à 17 heures. || from 8 to 5
:::

::: exercise Which tense?
J'habite ici depuis trois ans. — present or past? || Present. *Depuis* + still true = present tense.
J'ai vécu là pendant trois ans. — present or past? || Past. It's over.
Je suis parti il y a trois ans. — present or past? || Past. *Il y a* = ago.
J'attendais depuis vingt minutes. — what does it mean? || I had been waiting for twenty minutes.
:::

::: exercise dans or en?
Je pars ___ une heure. (an hour from now) || dans
J'ai fini ___ une heure. (it took me an hour) || en
Le train arrive ___ dix minutes. || dans
Il a écrit le livre ___ six mois. || en
:::
