# L'imparfait

The imperfect is the second past tense, and mechanically it is the easiest verb form in French:
**one** stem rule, **one** set of endings, **one** irregular verb in the entire language.

## Building it

Take the **nous** form of the present tense, drop **-ons**, add the endings.

| Pronoun | Ending |
|---|---|
| je | **-ais** |
| tu | **-ais** |
| il / elle | **-ait** |
| nous | **-ions** |
| vous | **-iez** |
| ils / elles | **-aient** |

| Infinitive | nous form | Stem | *je* form |
|---|---|---|---|
| parler | nous **parl**ons | parl- | je parlais |
| finir | nous **finiss**ons | finiss- | je finissais |
| vendre | nous **vend**ons | vend- | je vendais |
| faire | nous **fais**ons | fais- | je faisais |
| prendre | nous **pren**ons | pren- | je prenais |
| boire | nous **buv**ons | buv- | je buvais |
| voir | nous **voy**ons | voy- | je voyais |
| avoir | nous **av**ons | av- | j'avais |
| aller | nous **all**ons | all- | j'allais |

::: rule One exception: être
*Être* has no **-ons** stem to work from, so it uses **ét-**:

*j'étais, tu étais, il était, nous étions, vous étiez, ils étaient*

That is the only irregular imperfect in French. Every other verb, including the wildest
irregulars, follows the *nous* rule exactly.
:::

## Pronunciation

Four of the six forms — *-ais, -ais, -ait, -aient* — are all /ɛ/. They are indistinguishable in
speech. Only *nous* /jɔ̃/ and *vous* /je/ stand apart.

::: warning je parlais vs je parlerai
The imperfect *je parlais* is /paʁlɛ/. The future *je parlerai* is /paʁləʁe/. The **r** is the
difference, and it is the only difference. Listen for it.
:::

## Spelling adjustments

Verbs in **-ger** and **-cer** keep their softening letter before **a**, which means everywhere
except *nous* and *vous*.

::: examples
manger || je mangeais, tu mangeais, il mangeait, ils mangeaient — but nous mangions
commencer || je commençais, il commençait — but nous commencions
:::

And verbs whose stem already ends in **-i** produce a double i in *nous* and *vous*:
*étudier → nous étudiions*, *rire → nous riions*. It looks wrong and is correct.

## What the imperfect means

The imperfect describes rather than narrates. It answers *what was going on?*, never *what
happened next?*

### 1. Ongoing background action

::: examples
Je lisais quand tu es arrivé. || I was reading when you arrived.
Il pleuvait. || It was raining.
Elle dormait encore à midi. || She was still asleep at noon.
:::

### 2. Habits and repeated actions in the past

::: examples
Quand j'étais petit, j'allais à la mer tous les étés. || When I was little, I used to go to the sea every summer.
On mangeait toujours à huit heures. || We always used to eat at eight.
Il fumait beaucoup. || He used to smoke a lot.
:::

### 3. Description — states, weather, age, feelings, appearance

::: examples
Il faisait beau. || The weather was nice.
Elle avait vingt ans. || She was twenty.
J'étais fatigué. || I was tired.
La maison était grande et blanche. || The house was big and white.
Il y avait beaucoup de monde. || There were a lot of people.
:::

::: rule The verbs that live in the imperfect
*être*, *avoir*, *savoir*, *penser*, *croire*, *vouloir*, *pouvoir*, *aimer* and other verbs of
state naturally take the imperfect when talking about the past, because states last rather than
happen.

*J'**étais** content*, *je **savais***, *je **voulais** partir*, *il **y avait***.
:::

## Two idioms

**si + imperfect** makes a suggestion:

::: examples
Si on allait au cinéma ? || How about going to the cinema?
Si tu venais avec nous ? || Why don't you come with us?
:::

**être en train de** in the imperfect insists on the moment:

::: examples
J'étais en train de manger quand il a appelé. || I was in the middle of eating when he called.
:::

## Practice

::: exercise Conjugate in the imperfect
je (parler) || parlais
nous (finir) || finissions
ils (vendre) || vendaient
je (être) || étais — the only irregular
tu (avoir) || avais
il (faire) || faisait
nous (prendre) || prenions
vous (boire) || buviez
elle (aller) || allait
je (manger) || mangeais — the e stays before a
nous (manger) || mangions — and disappears here
il (commencer) || commençait
nous (étudier) || étudiions — double i
:::

::: exercise Why the imperfect?
*Il pleuvait.* || Description — the weather, a state.
*Quand j'étais petit, j'allais à la mer.* || Habit — a repeated past action.
*Je lisais quand tu es arrivé.* || Background — ongoing when something interrupted.
*Elle avait vingt ans.* || Description — age is a state.
*Il y avait beaucoup de monde.* || Description of a scene.
:::

It was raining. || Il pleuvait.

How about going to the cinema? || Si on allait au cinéma ?
There were a lot of people. || Il y avait beaucoup de monde.
When I was little, I was afraid of dogs. || Quand j'étais petit, j'avais peur des chiens.
:::
