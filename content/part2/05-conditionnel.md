# Le conditionnel

The conditional is *would*. It is also the politeness tense, the rumour tense, and half of every
hypothetical sentence in French. Better still, you already know how to build it.

## Present conditional

> **the future stem** + **the imperfect endings**

That is the whole rule. Every stem you learned for the future works here unchanged.

| Pronoun | Ending | *parler* | *être* | *avoir* | *aller* |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| je | **-ais** | parlerais | serais | aurais | irais |
| tu | **-ais** | parlerais | serais | aurais | irais |
| il / elle | **-ait** | parlerait | serait | aurait | irait |
| nous | **-ions** | parlerions | serions | aurions | irions |
| vous | **-iez** | parleriez | seriez | auriez | iriez |
| ils / elles | **-aient** | parleraient | seraient | auraient | iraient |

::: rule Future stem + imperfect endings
- Future: *je parler**ai***
- Conditional: *je parler**ais***

One letter apart in writing, and clearly different in speech: /paʁləʁe/ versus /paʁləʁɛ/.
:::

The irregular stems are identical to the future ones: *ser-, aur-, ir-, fer-, viendr-, verr-,
pourr-, voudr-, devr-, saur-, faudr-, enverr-, courr-, mourr-*.

## What it does

### 1. Politeness

This is the most common everyday use. The conditional turns a demand into a request.

::: examples
Je voudrais un café. || I'd like a coffee. — never *je veux un café* in a shop
Pourriez-vous m'aider ? || Could you help me?
Auriez-vous l'heure ? || Would you have the time?
J'aimerais réserver une table. || I'd like to book a table.
:::

::: warning je voudrais, not je veux
*Je veux un café* is grammatically perfect and socially blunt — it lands like *I want a coffee*
barked at a waiter. **Je voudrais** is the normal form. This single substitution does more for
how you come across than almost anything else in the language.
:::

### 2. Hypotheses — the *would* of unreal situations

::: examples
Je partirais bien en vacances. || I'd quite like to go on holiday.
Ce serait génial. || That would be great.
À ta place, je ne dirais rien. || In your position, I'd say nothing.
:::

### 3. The main clause of a si-sentence

::: examples
Si j'avais de l'argent, j'achèterais une maison. || If I had money, I'd buy a house.
Si tu venais, on serait content. || If you came, we'd be happy.
:::

The full pattern is the next chapter. The rule to hold onto now: **the conditional goes in the
other half, never after *si*.**

### 4. Unverified information

Journalism and gossip use the conditional to report something not confirmed.

::: examples
Il y aurait vingt blessés. || There are reportedly twenty injured.
Le président serait malade. || The president is said to be ill.
Elle aurait quitté son poste. || She has apparently left her job.
:::

English does this with *reportedly* or *apparently*; French does it with a tense.

### 5. Softened advice, with devoir and pouvoir

::: examples
Tu devrais te reposer. || You should rest.
Vous pourriez essayer. || You could try.
On devrait partir maintenant. || We ought to leave now.
:::

::: rule devrais = should, pourrais = could
*devoir* in the conditional is exactly English *should*.
*pouvoir* in the conditional is exactly English *could*.

These two forms carry an enormous amount of everyday conversation.
:::

## Past conditional

> **avoir** or **être** in the **conditional** + past participle

Meaning: *would have done*.

| Pronoun | with *avoir* | with *être* |
|---|---|---|
| je | j'aurais mangé | je serais allé(e) |
| tu | tu aurais mangé | tu serais allé(e) |
| il / elle | il aurait mangé | elle serait allée |
| nous | nous aurions mangé | nous serions allé(e)s |
| vous | vous auriez mangé | vous seriez allé(e)s |
| ils / elles | ils auraient mangé | elles seraient allées |

::: examples
J'aurais aimé venir. || I would have liked to come.
Ç'aurait été plus simple. || That would have been simpler.
Il serait parti plus tôt. || He would have left earlier.
Elles seraient venues. || They would have come.
:::

### Regret and reproach

::: examples
J'aurais dû te le dire. || I should have told you.
Tu aurais pu m'appeler ! || You could have called me!
On aurait dû partir plus tôt. || We should have left earlier.
J'aurais voulu être là. || I would have liked to be there.
:::

::: rule j'aurais dû = I should have
The single most useful past-conditional phrase. *Devoir* in the past conditional is English
*should have*; *pouvoir* is *could have*.

*J'aurais dû* — I should have. *Tu aurais pu* — you could have.
:::

### With si

::: examples
Si j'avais su, je serais venu. || If I had known, I would have come.
Si tu m'avais appelé, j'aurais répondu. || If you had called, I'd have answered.
:::

___ -vous m'aider ? (polite) || Pourriez

:::

:::

I should have told you. || J'aurais dû te le dire.
You could have called. || Tu aurais pu appeler.
I would have liked to come. || J'aurais aimé venir.
We should have left earlier. || On aurait dû partir plus tôt.
If I had known, I would have come. || Si j'avais su, je serais venu.
:::

::: exercise What is the conditional doing here?
*Le président serait malade.* || Reporting unverified information — "is said to be ill".
*Tu devrais dormir.* || Advice — "you should".
*Je voudrais un thé.* || Politeness.
*Si j'étais riche, je voyagerais.* || The main clause of a hypothetical.
:::
