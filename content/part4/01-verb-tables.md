# Irregular verb tables

Every verb in this course, in every tense it covers. Use it for lookup rather than reading.

**How to read a row:** the *je* form is given for each tense unless the verb is impersonal.
Where a verb has two present-tense stems, the *nous* form is shown too, because every other
tense derives from one or the other.

- The **imparfait** stem is always the *nous* present, minus **-ons**. (Only *être* breaks this.)
- The **conditionnel** is always the future stem plus imperfect endings.
- The **subjonctif** stem is usually the *ils* present, minus **-ent**.

## The four essentials

|---|---|

| Imparfait | j'étais · nous étions · ils étaient |

## The être verbs, in one place

Every reflexive verb, plus these:

*aller · venir · arriver · partir · entrer · sortir · monter · descendre · naître · mourir ·
rester · tomber · retourner · revenir · devenir · rentrer · passer*

::: warning Six take avoir when they have a direct object
*monter · descendre · sortir · rentrer · retourner · passer*

*Je **suis** monté* (I went up) vs *j'**ai** monté les valises* (I carried the cases up).
:::
