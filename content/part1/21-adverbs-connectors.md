# Adverbs, frequency and connectors

Two things that turn correct sentences into fluent ones: adverbs, which colour a verb, and
connectors, which stitch sentences into an argument. Both are largely vocabulary, so this
chapter is organised for lookup as much as for reading.

# Adverbs

An adverb modifies a verb, an adjective or another adverb. It **never** agrees with anything —
adverbs are invariable.

## Where the adverb goes

| Structure | Position | Example |
|---|---|---|
| simple tense | **after** the verb | *Il parle **lentement**.* |
| compound tense, short adverb | **between** auxiliary and participle | *J'ai **bien** mangé.* |
| compound tense, long adverb | **after** the participle | *J'ai répondu **immédiatement**.* |
| modifying an adjective | before it | ***très** grand* |
| time or place adverb | often at either end | ***Hier**, je suis sorti.* · *Je suis sorti **hier**.* |

::: warning Never put the adverb between subject and verb
English says *I **often** eat there*. French cannot: **Je mange **souvent** là**, never
*je souvent mange*. This is one of the most reliable tells of an English speaker.
:::

Short adverbs that sit inside a compound tense: *bien, mal, déjà, encore, toujours, souvent,
jamais, trop, beaucoup, vraiment, presque, peut-être*.

::: examples
J'ai déjà mangé. || I've already eaten.
Il n'a jamais compris. || He never understood.
Elle a beaucoup travaillé. || She worked a lot.
Nous avons presque fini. || We've almost finished.
:::

## Frequency

| French | English |
|---|---|
| **toujours** | always |
| **souvent** | often |
| **fréquemment** | frequently |
| **d'habitude**, **habituellement** | usually |
| **généralement** | generally |
| **parfois**, **quelquefois** | sometimes |
| **de temps en temps** | from time to time |
| **rarement** | rarely |
| **presque jamais** | almost never |
| **ne … jamais** | never |
| **tous les jours** | every day |
| **une fois par semaine** | once a week |
| **deux fois par mois** | twice a month |

::: rule __ fois par __
The pattern for any rate: **une fois par jour**, **trois fois par semaine**, **deux fois par
an**. Note *par*, not *dans* or *à*.
:::

::: examples
Je vais à la piscine deux fois par semaine. || twice a week
Il est presque toujours en retard. || almost always late
Elle ne vient presque jamais. || she almost never comes
De temps en temps, on sort. || now and then
:::

## Time

| Past | Present | Future |
|---|---|---|
| hier — yesterday | aujourd'hui — today | demain — tomorrow |
| avant-hier — the day before yesterday | maintenant — now | après-demain — the day after tomorrow |
| hier soir — last night | en ce moment — right now | le lendemain — the next day |
| récemment — recently | déjà — already | bientôt — soon |
| autrefois — in the past | encore — still | tout à l'heure — in a while |
| tout à l'heure — a while ago | toujours — still | désormais — from now on |

::: warning Words that point both ways
**tout à l'heure** means *a while ago* with a past verb and *in a while* with a future one.
**encore** means *still* (*il dort encore*) or *again* (*encore une fois*).
**toujours** means *always* or *still* (*il habite toujours ici* — he still lives here).
:::

## Manner, degree and place

| Manner | Degree | Place |
|---|---|---|
| bien — well | très — very | ici — here |
| mal — badly | trop — too | là — there |
| vite — fast | assez — enough, quite | là-bas — over there |
| lentement — slowly | peu — little | partout — everywhere |
| ensemble — together | un peu — a little | ailleurs — elsewhere |
| exprès — on purpose | beaucoup — a lot | dehors — outside |
| debout — standing | presque — almost | dedans — inside |
| soudain — suddenly | tellement — so much | loin — far |
| vraiment — really | plutôt — rather | près — near |

::: warning bien / mal are adverbs; bon / mauvais are adjectives
*Il chante **bien**.* — he sings well. (adverb, modifies the verb)
*C'est un **bon** chanteur.* — he's a good singer. (adjective, modifies the noun)

*C'est **bon*** describes a thing (food, a result). *C'est **bien*** approves of a situation.
:::

::: warning Two false friends worth flagging
**actuellement** means *currently*, never *actually*. For *actually*, say **en fait**.
**éventuellement** means *possibly*, never *eventually*. For *eventually*, say **finalement**.
:::

Full adverb formation from adjectives — the **-ment** family — has its own chapter in Part II.

# Connectors

Connectors carry the logic of what you are saying. Grouping them by function is far more useful
than an alphabetical list.

## Sequence

| French | English |
|---|---|
| **d'abord** | first of all |
| **premièrement** | firstly |
| **ensuite**, **puis** | then, next |
| **après** | afterwards |
| **enfin**, **finalement** | finally |
| **au début** | at the beginning |
| **à la fin** | in the end |
| **entre-temps** | in the meantime |

## Cause

| French | English | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **parce que** | because | the general-purpose one |
| **car** | for, because | slightly formal; never starts a sentence |
| **puisque** | since (a known reason) | |
| **comme** | as, since | only at the start of a sentence |
| **grâce à** | thanks to | positive outcome |
| **à cause de** | because of | negative outcome |
| **en raison de** | owing to | formal, neutral |
| **en tant que** | as, in the capacity of | |

::: examples
Je suis en retard parce qu'il y a une grève. || the everyday choice
Comme il pleut, on reste ici. || *comme* opens the sentence
Grâce à toi, j'ai réussi. || positive
À cause de la grève, je suis en retard. || negative
:::

## Consequence

| French | English |
|---|---|
| **donc** | so, therefore |
| **alors** | so, then |
| **du coup** | so, as a result (very common in speech) |
| **par conséquent** | consequently (formal) |
| **c'est pourquoi** | that's why |
| **ainsi** | thus |

## Contrast

| French | English | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **mais** | but | |
| **pourtant**, **cependant** | however, yet | |
| **quand même** | still, anyway | |
| **malgré** | despite | + noun |
| **bien que**, **quoique** | although | + **subjunctive** |
| **même si** | even if | + indicative |
| **au lieu de** | instead of | + infinitive |
| **par contre**, **en revanche** | on the other hand | |
| **alors que**, **tandis que** | whereas | |

::: warning bien que takes the subjunctive; même si does not
***Bien qu'**il **soit** fatigué, il travaille.* — subjunctive.
***Même s'**il **est** fatigué, il travaille.* — indicative.

They mean nearly the same thing and behave differently. If you have not met the subjunctive
yet, *même si* is the safe choice.
:::

## Addition and explanation

| French | English |
|---|---|
| **et** | and |
| **aussi**, **également** | also |
| **de plus**, **en plus** | moreover |
| **ainsi que** | as well as |
| **outre** | besides, in addition to |
| **c'est-à-dire** | that is to say |
| **autrement dit** | in other words |
| **par exemple** | for example |
| **tel que** | such as |
| **en fait** | in fact, actually |
| **justement** | exactly, precisely |
| **notamment** | notably, in particular |

## Condition and alternative

| French | English |
|---|---|
| **si** | if |
| **au cas où** | in case (+ conditional) |
| **à moins que** | unless (+ subjunctive) |
| **sauf** | except |
| **ou** | or |
| **soit … soit** | either … or |
| **ne … ni … ni** | neither … nor |
| **sinon** | otherwise, if not |

## Summing up

| French | English |
|---|---|
| **bref** | in short |
| **pour faire court** | long story short |
| **en résumé** | to sum up |
| **de toute façon** | anyway |
| **en tout cas** | in any case |
| **à part ça** | apart from that |
| **d'ailleurs** | besides, incidentally |
| **finalement** | in the end |

::: tip The four that make you sound native
**du coup** (so, as a result), **en fait** (actually), **quand même** (still, anyway) and
**d'ailleurs** (besides) are everywhere in spoken French and almost absent from textbooks.
Slipping them into speech changes how you sound more than any amount of extra vocabulary.
:::

## Practice

::: exercise Where does the adverb go?
(souvent) Je mange ici. || Je mange souvent ici. — never *je souvent mange*
(déjà) J'ai mangé. || J'ai déjà mangé. — inside the compound tense
(immédiatement) J'ai répondu. || J'ai répondu immédiatement. — long adverb, after the participle
(bien) Il a dormi. || Il a bien dormi.
(hier) Je suis sorti. || Hier, je suis sorti. / Je suis sorti hier.
(très) Elle est intelligente. || Elle est très intelligente.
:::

::: exercise bon or bien?
Il chante ___. || bien — modifying a verb
C'est un ___ chanteur. || bon — modifying a noun
Ce gâteau est ___. || bon — describing a thing
C'est ___ de faire du sport. || bien — approving of an activity
:::

::: exercise Which connector?
___ il pleut, on reste ici. (since, opening the sentence) || Comme
Je suis en retard ___ il y a une grève. || parce qu'
___ toi, j'ai réussi. (positive) || Grâce à
___ la grève, je suis en retard. (negative) || À cause de
Il est fatigué, ___ il travaille. (however) || pourtant
___ il soit fatigué, il travaille. (although + subjunctive) || Bien qu'
___ , je pense que c'est une bonne idée. (in short) || Bref
Il pleut, ___ on ne sort pas. (so) || du coup / donc
:::

::: exercise False friends
*Actuellement, je travaille à Paris.* || Currently — not "actually".
How do you say "actually"? || En fait.
*Éventuellement, on pourrait partir.* || Possibly — not "eventually".
How do you say "eventually"? || Finalement.
:::
