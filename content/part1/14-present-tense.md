# The present tense

French has one present tense where English has three. *Je parle* covers *I speak*, *I am
speaking* and *I do speak*. There is no continuous form to learn — which makes this the
easiest tense in the language, once the endings are automatic.

## The three regular families

Verbs are sorted by their infinitive ending.

### -er verbs (parler)

By far the largest group — roughly ninety per cent of all French verbs, and every new verb
entering the language joins it.

| Pronoun | Ending | *parler* |
|---|---|---|
| je | **-e** | parle |
| tu | **-es** | parles |
| il / elle / on | **-e** | parle |
| nous | **-ons** | parlons |
| vous | **-ez** | parlez |
| ils / elles | **-ent** | parlent |

::: warning Four of these six sound identical
*parle*, *parles*, *parle*, *parlent* are all /paʁl/. Only *parlons* /paʁlɔ̃/ and *parlez*
/paʁle/ differ. The written endings matter enormously; the spoken ones barely exist.
:::

### -ir verbs (finir)

| Pronoun | Ending | *finir* |
|---|---|---|
| je | **-is** | finis |
| tu | **-is** | finis |
| il / elle / on | **-it** | finit |
| nous | **-issons** | finissons |
| vous | **-issez** | finissez |
| ils / elles | **-issent** | finissent |

Others in this family: *choisir*, *réussir*, *remplir*, *punir*, *réfléchir*, *grandir*,
*enrichir*, *obéir*.

### -re verbs (vendre)

| Pronoun | Ending | *vendre* |
|---|---|---|
| je | **-s** | vends |
| tu | **-s** | vends |
| il / elle / on | **—** | vend |
| nous | **-ons** | vendons |
| vous | **-ez** | vendez |
| ils / elles | **-ent** | vendent |

Others: *attendre*, *entendre*, *descendre*, *répondre*, *perdre*, *rendre*, *mordre*.

## The four essential irregulars

If you learn only four verbs, learn these. Between them they build most of the tense system.

| Pronoun | **être** (to be) | **avoir** (to have) | **aller** (to go) | **faire** (to do/make) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| je / j' | suis | ai | vais | fais |
| tu | es | as | vas | fais |
| il / elle / on | est | a | va | fait |
| nous | sommes | avons | allons | faisons |
| vous | êtes | avez | allez | faites |
| ils / elles | sont | ont | vont | font |

::: warning Three forms that break the pattern
*vous **êtes***, *vous **faites***, *vous **dites*** are the only three verbs in the language
whose *vous* form does not end in **-ez**.

And *nous **faisons*** is pronounced /fəzɔ̃/ — the *ai* is said /ə/, not /ɛ/. It is the only
verb that does this.
:::

## The -ir verbs that behave like -er verbs

A second, smaller **-ir** group drops a consonant instead of adding **-iss-**. *Partir* and
*dormir* are the models: remove the last three letters of the infinitive, then add the endings.

| Pronoun | *partir* | *dormir* | *sortir* | *sentir* | *servir* |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| je | pars | dors | sors | sens | sers |
| tu | pars | dors | sors | sens | sers |
| il / elle / on | part | dort | sort | sent | sert |
| nous | partons | dormons | sortons | sentons | servons |
| vous | partez | dormez | sortez | sentez | servez |
| ils / elles | partent | dorment | sortent | sentent | servent |

The pattern: the singular loses the consonant (*par-s*), the plural keeps it (*part-ons*).
Also here: *mentir*, *courir*, *ouvrir*, *offrir*, *souffrir* — though the last three take
**-er** endings in the singular (*j'ouvre*, *j'offre*).

## Other high-frequency irregulars

| Pronoun | **pouvoir** | **vouloir** | **devoir** | **prendre** | **venir** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| je | peux | veux | dois | prends | viens |
| tu | peux | veux | dois | prends | viens |
| il / elle / on | peut | veut | doit | prend | vient |
| nous | pouvons | voulons | devons | prenons | venons |
| vous | pouvez | voulez | devez | prenez | venez |
| ils / elles | peuvent | veulent | doivent | prennent | viennent |

| Pronoun | **savoir** | **voir** | **dire** | **lire** | **écrire** | **boire** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| je | sais | vois | dis | lis | écris | bois |
| tu | sais | vois | dis | lis | écris | bois |
| il / elle / on | sait | voit | dit | lit | écrit | boit |
| nous | savons | voyons | disons | lisons | écrivons | buvons |
| vous | savez | voyez | **dites** | lisez | écrivez | buvez |
| ils / elles | savent | voient | disent | lisent | écrivent | boivent |

::: tip The shape of a French irregular verb
Nearly all of them follow the same silhouette: **the three singular forms and *ils* share one
stem; *nous* and *vous* share another.**

*je peux, tu peux, il peut* / *nous pouvons, vous pouvez* / *ils peuvent*
*je viens, tu viens, il vient* / *nous venons, vous venez* / *ils viennent*

Learn the *je* form and the *nous* form and you can usually reconstruct the rest.
:::

## Spelling changes in -er verbs

These verbs are regular in sound but adjust their spelling to stay pronounceable.

| Change | Why | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **-ger** → **-geons** (nous) | keeps /ʒ/ before *o* | *nous mangeons*, *nous nageons*, *nous voyageons*, *nous partageons* |
| **-cer** → **-çons** (nous) | keeps /s/ before *o* | *nous commençons*, *nous lançons*, *nous avançons* |
| **e_er** → **è** | an unstressed *e* would vanish | *acheter → j'achète*, *lever → je lève*, *emmener → j'emmène* |
| **é_er** → **è** | same reason | *préférer → je préfère*, *espérer → j'espère*, *répéter → je répète* |
| **-eler / -eter** → double consonant | same reason | *appeler → j'appelle*, *jeter → je jette* |
| **-yer** → **-i-** | before a silent ending | *payer → je paie*, *envoyer → j'envoie*, *nettoyer → je nettoie* |

::: rule The pattern behind all of them
Every one of these changes happens in the forms where the ending is **silent** — *je*, *tu*,
*il*, *ils* — and disappears in *nous* and *vous*, where the ending is pronounced.

*j'achète*, *tu achètes*, *il achète*, *ils achètent* — but *nous achetons*, *vous achetez*.
:::

## What the present tense covers

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| right now | *Je mange.* — I'm eating. |
| habits | *Je mange à midi.* — I eat at noon. |
| general truths | *L'eau bout à 100 degrés.* |
| the near future | *Je pars demain.* — I'm leaving tomorrow. |
| something ongoing since the past | *J'habite ici **depuis** 2019.* — I **have** lived here since 2019. |

::: warning depuis takes the present, not the past
English says *I **have** lived here for three years*. French says *J'habite ici **depuis** trois
ans* — present tense, because you still live there. Using the past here is one of the most
persistent English-speaker errors.
:::

If you need to insist that something is happening at this exact moment, use **être en train
de** + infinitive: *Je suis en train de manger* — I'm in the middle of eating.
