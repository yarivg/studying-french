# Le futur simple

The future is the tidiest tense in French: **one** set of endings for every verb in the
language, with no exceptions at all. The only thing that varies is the stem.

## The endings

They are the present tense of **avoir** — *ai, as, a, (av)ons, (av)ez, ont* — stuck onto the
infinitive.

| Pronoun | Ending |
|---|---|
| je | **-ai** |
| tu | **-as** |
| il / elle / on | **-a** |
| nous | **-ons** |
| vous | **-ez** |
| ils / elles | **-ont** |

## Regular stems

- **-er** and **-ir** verbs: the stem is the whole infinitive.
- **-re** verbs: drop the final **-e** first.

| Pronoun | *parler* | *finir* | *vendre* |
|---|---|---|---|
| je | parlerai | finirai | vendrai |
| tu | parleras | finiras | vendras |
| il / elle / on | parlera | finira | vendra |
| nous | parlerons | finirons | vendrons |
| vous | parlerez | finirez | vendrez |
| ils / elles | parleront | finiront | vendront |

::: rule The r is the signal
Every future form contains an **r** immediately before the ending. If you hear /ʁ/ in that
position, you are hearing a future — this is what distinguishes *je parlerai* (future) from
*je parlais* (imperfect) in speech.
:::

## Irregular stems

The endings never change. Only these stems have to be learned.

| Infinitive | Future stem | *je* form |
|---|---|---|
| **être** | ser- | je serai |
| **avoir** | aur- | j'aurai |
| **aller** | ir- | j'irai |
| **faire** | fer- | je ferai |
| **venir** | viendr- | je viendrai |
| **tenir** | tiendr- | je tiendrai |
| **voir** | verr- | je verrai |
| **envoyer** | enverr- | j'enverrai |
| **pouvoir** | pourr- | je pourrai |
| **vouloir** | voudr- | je voudrai |
| **devoir** | devr- | je devrai |
| **savoir** | saur- | je saurai |
| **recevoir** | recevr- | je recevrai |
| **falloir** | faudr- | il faudra |
| **pleuvoir** | pleuvr- | il pleuvra |
| **valoir** | vaudr- | il vaudra |
| **courir** | courr- | je courrai |
| **mourir** | mourr- | je mourrai |

The four you will use constantly, in full:

| Pronoun | **être** | **avoir** | **aller** | **venir** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| je / j' | serai | aurai | irai | viendrai |
| tu | seras | auras | iras | viendras |
| il / elle / on | sera | aura | ira | viendra |
| nous | serons | aurons | irons | viendrons |
| vous | serez | aurez | irez | viendrez |
| ils / elles | seront | auront | iront | viendront |

::: warning Double r changes the sound
*courir → **courrai***, *mourir → **mourrai***, *voir → **verrai***, *pouvoir → **pourrai***,
*envoyer → **enverrai***. The doubled r is audibly longer, and it is the only thing separating
*je courais* (imperfect, I was running) from *je courrai* (future, I will run).
:::

## Spelling-change verbs

The **e → è** shift from the present tense carries into the future, and it applies to **every**
person, because the ending is silent throughout the singular anyway.

::: examples
acheter → j'achèterai, nous achèterons || the è appears in all six forms
appeler → j'appellerai || double l throughout
jeter → je jetterai || double t throughout
payer → je paierai || y becomes i
:::

Verbs in **é** are the exception: *préférer → je préférerai*, keeping the *é*.

## When to use it

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| a plain future fact | *Demain, il **fera** beau.* |
| a promise or intention | *Je te **rappellerai**.* |
| a prediction | *Tu **comprendras** un jour.* |
| a polite command | *Vous **fermerez** la porte, s'il vous plaît.* |
| after *quand*, *lorsque*, *dès que* | *Quand j'**aurai** le temps, je t'**appellerai**.* |

::: rule quand takes the future in French
English uses the present after *when*: *When I **have** time, I'll call you.*
French uses the future in **both** halves: *Quand j'**aurai** le temps, je t'**appellerai**.*

The same applies after **lorsque**, **dès que** (as soon as), **aussitôt que**, **tant que** (as
long as). Using the present here is a very common English-speaker error.
:::

## Futur simple vs futur proche

French has a second future — **aller** + infinitive — which is what people actually use most of
the time.

| | Futur proche | Futur simple |
|---|---|---|
| Form | *Je **vais** partir* | *Je **partirai*** |
| Feel | near, planned, certain | distant, formal, or a plain statement |
| Register | spoken | written and spoken |

::: examples
Je vais partir dans cinq minutes. || imminent — futur proche is natural
Je partirai en France l'année prochaine. || distant — futur simple fits
Attention, tu vas tomber ! || immediate — only the futur proche works here
Un jour, je comprendrai. || a general prediction
:::

::: tip When in doubt, use the futur proche
In conversation, **aller + infinitive** is the default and always sounds fine. The futur simple
is never *wrong*, but overusing it in speech sounds slightly written. In an essay, the reverse
applies.
:::

## Le futur antérieur

Once you have the future, the *future perfect* is free: **avoir** or **être** in the future +
past participle. It means *will have done*.

::: examples
J'aurai fini avant midi. || I'll have finished before noon.
Quand tu arriveras, je serai déjà parti. || When you arrive, I'll already have left.
Dès qu'il aura mangé, on partira. || As soon as he's eaten, we'll leave.
:::

Auxiliary choice and participle agreement follow exactly the same rules as the passé composé.
