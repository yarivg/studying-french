## Error themes, ranked

1. **Production, not recognition.** Multiple choice is solid; building a sentence
   from English is where it leaks.
2. **`avoir mal à`** — the person is the subject, not the body part. Wrote
   `m'estomac a mal`, `ses dents a mal`. Took two passes.
3. **Numbers** — `soixante et onze` (71 is the last `et`), `quatre-vingts` keeps
   its -s only when nothing follows, `cent` loses its -s when a number follows.
4. **`aucun`** — attaches straight to the noun, no partitive, and agrees.
5. **`celle de` vs `la sienne`** — owner named with `de` takes a demonstrative.
6. **-er spelling changes** — `je jette` / `nous appelons`, `j'achète` / `nous achetons`.
7. **Stacking negatives** — `n'a jamais rien vu`.

## Grading rules the pages settled on

Ignore accents, cédille, apostrophes and hyphens. Accept the gap filler or the
whole sentence. Lists are order-insensitive. No pronunciation or IPA questions.
