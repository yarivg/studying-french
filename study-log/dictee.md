right, spelled wrong), the same family as the production drills.

heard twice (`s'ennuie` for `s'ennuyait`, `pense` for `pensait`), `se` for `s'est`,

4. Silent plural: `était les yeux` (`étaient`, subject after the verb).

## Dictée 5

Same podcast, 46:46 to 51:30 (`&t=2806s`). Cold watch at 1x: **60%** understood (30% on the
first clip of 2026-10-20). 0/12 first time, but three were one word off (`interrese`, `dit-nous`,
`gents`). First dictée with word errors counted: 34, 2.8 per sentence.

**Heard wrong**:
- `en` lost again: `de son sortir` for `de s'en sortir`. Fourth time in three dictées.
- Small words in the flow: `de tout` (`du tout`), `comme` for `qu'on`, `comment` for `comme`,
  `majorité podcasteurs` (dropped `de`), `de les` for `d'aller`, `on etait l'assiste` for
  `on est allés assister`.
- `ce sera` for `ce serait` (futur for conditionnel: one sound apart).
- Known word lost at speed: `de pays tres lentent` for `depuis très longtemps`.
- Unknown word: `chouette` (`souhaite`).

**Silent grammar**, ranked:
1. `-é` vs `-er`: `avait changer`, `je suis tomber`. The participle after `avoir / être`.
2. `leur rencontrer`, `leur fréquenter` (`les`): `leur` has an `r` the audio did not have. The
   grammar reflex overrode the ear: `rencontrer / fréquenter qqn` are direct. Top theme.
3. `les plans` for `de plan`: `changer de` + bare noun.
4. `dit-nous` (`dites-nous`): the `vous` imperative.
5. Spelling: `gents`, `interrese`, `medians`, `ministers` (English), `financecierment`,
   `vennaient`, `de vantage`.

Redo: 6 of 12 on the first pass. Survivors: `majorité podcasteurs` three passes running (the
`de` after a quantity noun: `une majorité de`, `beaucoup de`, `la plupart des`), `je suis tomber`,
`gents`, `ministers`, `de tout`, `financierment`. The `-é` / `-er` slip survived the correction
once: it is a writing reflex, not hearing.

Right first time: `qui venaient`, `qui font` (plural after `qui`, both), `tous les médias / tous
les ministères`, `ce que vous en pensez`, `s'il n'y a que`, `décidé de`, `essayer de`,
`prévoit de`, `envie de`.

## Dictée 6

New video, new speaker (a debate coach, faster and denser than the #103 hosts), 0:51 to 5:05
(`&t=51s`). Cold watch at 1x: **60%**, on a first contact. 1/12 first time, three more one word
off (`est` for `et`, `noter`, `jetter`).

**Heard wrong**:
- Fixed phrases not known, so not heard: `il vaut mieux` (`ils vont mieux`), `en main`
  (`cartomas`), `le simple fait` (`les samples`), `ça ne sert à rien` (`ça un`), `cohérent`.
- `se bloquer` heard as `c'est bloqué`; `n'essaie même pas de dire` lost entirely.
- `on n'a` written as `n'ont`: the subject `on` dropped, then the verb matched to nothing.

**Silent grammar**, ranked:
1. `-é` / `-er` both ways: `être noter` (`noté`), `argumenter` (`argumenté`), `parlez` for the
   infinitive `parler`. Third dictée running.
2. Negated infinitive: `de n'aient pas avoir` (`de ne pas avoir`), `c'est ne pas donner` (`ce
   n'est pas donner`): `ne pas` goes together before an infinitive, around a conjugated verb.
3. Partitive after negation, over-applied: `pas avoir de bonne réponse`, `pas donner de bonne
   réponse` (key `les / des bonnes réponses`). `de` replaces `un / des` after a negation, not
   `les`. In dic-62 `de` is acceptable (widen).
4. Silent plural: `bonne reponse` for `bonnes réponses` twice, `les étrangers` for `l'étranger`.
5. Spelling: `etranges` (`étrangers`), `manifique` (`magnifique`), `jetter` (`jeter`; `je jette`
   doubles, the infinitive does not), `ce lui` (`celui`).

Comprehension at 1x after the loop: **80-85%** (60% cold).

Redo: all 11 fixed in one pass, the first dictée with no survivor. The gap was the unknown
phrases and checking, not knowledge.

Right first time: `avant de prendre la parole`, `plutôt que de ne pas parler du tout`, `ils n'ont
pas` (the swallowed `n'` written), `plus personne ne veut` twice, `c'est l'autre qui nous renvoie`,
`il faut ... se jeter ... et prendre`, `les Français ne supportent pas`.
