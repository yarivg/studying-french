## Dictée 1

Meaning came through in 10 of 12. Most errors were **silent grammar** (heard
right, spelled wrong), the same family as the production drills.

**Heard wrong** (5): `elle en` dropped (`le loup ? elle en avait peur`), `-ait` not
heard twice (`s'ennuie` for `s'ennuyait`, `pense` for `pensait`), `se` for `s'est`,
the whole `et ne leur dis` in a negative imperative.

**Silent grammar**, ranked:
1. Agreement and gender: `embrasse` (`embrassée`, preceding `l'`), `parti`
   (`partie`), `est devenu` (`devenue`, survived one redo), `le montagne`, `la loup`
   (survived one redo).
2. Same sound, other word: `ca` for `sa`, `l'a repare` for `la réparer`, `lave` for
   `laver` (infinitive after `de` / `à`).
3. Auxiliary: `a devenu` (`est devenue`), `n'ai pas rentrer` (`n'est pas rentrée`).
4. Silent plural: `était les yeux` (`étaient`, subject after the verb).
5. Spelling: `malheure`, `moll`, `cord`, `bous`, `tout les`.

Redo: 10 of 12 on the first pass; `est devenu(e)` and `le / la loup` took one more.
