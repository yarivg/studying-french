## Dictée 1

Meaning came through in 10 of 12. Most errors were **silent grammar** (heard
right, spelled wrong), the same family as the production drills.

**Heard wrong** (5): `elle en` dropped (`le loup ? elle en avait peur`), `-ait` not
heard twice (`s'ennuie` for `s'ennuyait`, `pense` for `pensait`), `se` for `s'est`,
the whole `et ne leur dis` in a negative imperative.

**Silent grammar**, ranked:
1. Agreement and gender: `embrasse` (`embrassée`, preceding `l'`), `parti`
   (`partie`), `est devenu` (`devenue`, survived one redo), `le montagne`, `la loup`
   (survived one redo).
2. Same sound, other word: `ca` for `sa`, `l'a repare` for `la réparer`, `lave` for
   `laver` (infinitive after `de` / `à`).
3. Auxiliary: `a devenu` (`est devenue`), `n'ai pas rentrer` (`n'est pas rentrée`).
4. Silent plural: `était les yeux` (`étaient`, subject after the verb).
5. Spelling: `malheure`, `moll`, `cord`, `bous`, `tout les`.

Redo: 10 of 12 on the first pass; `est devenu(e)` and `le / la loup` took one more.

## Dictée 3

The first dictée from real speech: podcast `cOdKYE8WnCk` from 37:00 to 42:00 (link
with `&t=2218s`), watched once at 1x before the drill. 3/12 right first time. Dictée 2
(Sophie ch. 2) is built but not yet sat.

**Heard wrong** (6): small words lost in the flow.
- `y` dropped twice: `de vivre` for `d'y vivre`, `je suis allé` for `j'y suis allé`.
- `ou six` heard as `aussi`.
- `aimaient m'y emmener` came out as `et ma mi omne`.
- `j'ai six piqûres en l'espace` came out as `je cipique a las spase`.
- `de luvre` for `du Louvre`.
- Unknown words broke the parse: `dizaine` (`deusieme`), `piqûre`, `emmener`.

**Silent grammar** (6), ranked:
1. Silent plural `s`, four times: `des bon plan`, `moins cher`, `les bonne chose`,
   `le cote negatif` (`les côtés négatifs`). `ailleurs` always takes its `s`.
2. `-er` after `faire`: `fait bouffe` (`bouffer`).
3. Gender: `la musée` (`le musée`: masculine despite `-ée`).
4. `toute` for the invariable pronoun `tout` (`tu as tout`).
5. Graded right but parsed wrong: `j'allais l'avoir` for `la voir`. It passed only
   because the grader drops apostrophes. Same sound, different words.

Redo: 10 of 12 on the first pass. Survivors: `du Louvre` (one more pass) and dic-28,
which took three more: `la musée` twice and the dropped `y` (`je suis allé` for
`j'y suis allé`) all three times. The `y` failure is now production as well as
listening: he no longer has the audio as an excuse. Feed it to the next production drill.
