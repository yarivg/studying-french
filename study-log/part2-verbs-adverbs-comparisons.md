## Error themes, ranked

1. **Fixed frames whose preposition is not the English one.** Heaviest, and the
   same family as theme 6 in CLAUDE.md.
   `conseiller à` -> `conseiller **de**`, `interdire **de**`, `arrêter **de**`,
   `passer + time + **à** + inf` (wrote `en lisant`), `au lieu de` (wrote
   `en lieu de`), `plutôt que **de**` (wrote `plus que`), `reprocher **à** qqn
   **de**`, `arriver **à**` (wrote `ne peux pas arrive`).

2. **English leaking into word choice.** A real word, wrong meaning, or a calque.
   `probité` for "forbade" (`interdit`), `dépenser` for time (`passer`),
   `gentilement` for "gently" (`doucement`), `d'ailleurs` for "otherwise"
   (`sinon`), `quitter fumer` (`arrêter de fumer`), `pas important` for "never
   mind" (`ce n'est pas grave`), `pas seulement moi` for "the only one"
   (`le seul`), `en le froid` (`par ce froid`).

3. **Negation slots.** `n'a fait pas` (the auxiliary is wrapped: `n'a jamais
   eu`), `de l'avoir jamais` (an infinitive needs `ne jamais`), `un accident`
   after a negation (`d'accident`), `pas encore` vs `toujours pas`.

4. **Spelling of the form, with the grammar right.** `arrete` for `tu arrêtes`
   in the subjunctive, `apprandrai`, `je pratiquer`, `elle parle` for the `tu`
   imperative `parle`, `la plus cher` for `chère`, `mes parent`, `le moindre
   idée` for `la moindre idée`, `ecouter` for the participle `écouté`.

5. **Tense follows the English too literally.** `n'avais` for "I haven't"
   (`n'ai`), `aurait peur` after `sinon` where the futur `aura` belongs.
   Clean on `quand` + futur and on `si` + plus-que-parfait this time.

6. **Structures English builds differently.** "The more..., the less..." is
   `plus je pratique, moins je fais d'erreurs`, with no `que`. Relative `dont`
   replaces `de + noun` (wrote `qui je t'ai dit`). Superlative + `du monde`
   (wrote `de toute le monde`).

## Closing sitting, 2026-10-11

Nine questions first answered at the end, none right first time. What failed:

1. **Missing `de` before an infinitive.** Three questions running:
   `dit me rappeler` (`dire à qqn de`), `oublié faire` (`oublier de`), `éviter
   être` (`éviter de`). The family: `décider / oublier / essayer / refuser /
   accepter / finir / arrêter / éviter de`. Against it, `hésiter à`, `commencer
   à`, `continuer à`.
2. **`lui` vs `le`.** `je l'ai dit` for `je lui ai dit`. The rule: ask whether the
   French verb takes `à` + person, never the English. `dire / demander /
   téléphoner / répondre à` -> `lui`. `appeler / rappeler / attendre / écouter /
   aider` -> `le`.
3. **Participles and conjugation.** `j'ai lis` (`lu`), `decided` (`décidé`),
   `il a oubli` (`oublié`), `on somme` (`on est` or `nous sommes`: `on` takes
   the 3rd person singular).
4. **Words.** `la station` for a train station (`la gare`), `malheuresement`
   (`malheureusement`, from `malheureuse`), `rappeller` (`rappeler`),
   `en haute-voix` (`fort`), `au bureau en le printemps` (`au printemps`).
5. **Backshift.** `j'ai lu qu'il a trouvé` for "had found": the earlier action
   inside a reported clause goes into the plus-que-parfait. The page accepted
   the passé composé; colloquial, and kept in the key. Tense choice was
   otherwise right: `était déjà parti` was correct, and on vac-14 he worked out
   unprompted that `avoir peur` is a background state (imparfait), not an
   earlier action.

## Bank and retest

`vac.bank.js` in this folder is the full 46-question bank in the french-drill
format, with the page's own keys, vocab hints and explanations. Variants are
widened for the keys that were too narrow. Ids are `vacf-NN` in page order,
`from` is the original `vac-NN` id where it is known (nine are not).

Each question carries `missed`:

- `sitting`: wrong in the 2026-10-10 sitting (16).
- `closing`: first answered 2026-10-11, wrong first time (9, vacf-38 to vacf-46).
- `earlier`: wrong in earlier sittings, right by the end (17).
- `null`: right first time (4).

To sit it: copy it to the scratchpad as `questions-vacf.js`, build the shell with
the french-drill skill (`key: 'drill-vac'`), deal `missed != null` first. Ask
for "retest vac" and it is built from this file. Retest after a few days, not
the same day. A pass means producing the form from English with no hint.
